Project: Magicly — AI-Powered Educational Platform

Magicly is an AI-driven learning platform that helps students study more efficiently. Students upload their course materials (PDFs, Word docs, or images), and the platform automatically:

Summarizes lecture content into key points
Generates flashcards for quick review
Creates quizzes based on the uploaded material
Builds a personalized study plan based on the subject and available time (day-by-day schedule)
Explains lessons in 4 different styles (simplified, academic, with analogies, or practical examples)
Supports both Arabic and English (technical terms stay as-is, explanations adapt to the user's preferred language)
Provides an AI study assistant/chatbot for Q&A based on the uploaded reference material

Built with Next.js (frontend). I'm sending a folder containing the project's code and files so you can review and help me edit/modify them.
